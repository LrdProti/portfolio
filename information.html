Own software

Most of this work runs through a desktop app I built for my studio. It puts image and video models from Higgsfield, fal and ComfyUI into one interface and keeps every result, prompt and cost in one history.

  • An editor for cuts, colour, voice-over and captions that line up with the voice
  • Garment transfer that keeps logos, prints and patches accurate
  • Consistent model characters across shoots
  • Background swaps that keep the original shadows
  • 3D previz in Blender before anything is generated
