Own software

I built my own software, SKINGEN STUDIO, to combine different image and video models with workflows I designed myself. They make production faster and more precise, and they give every project an individual look instead of a generic AI one. Editing runs in the same app: its own editor handles cuts, colour, voice-over and captions.

  • Garment transfer that keeps logos, prints and patches accurate
  • Consistent model characters across shoots
  • Background swaps that keep the original shadows
  • Captions that line up with the voice
  • 3D previz in Blender before anything is generated
